    About HypaSpeed

    HypaSpeed exists to answer one question well: how fast is this connection, right now — and can it actually keep up with what you throw at it?

    We measure real transfers, not estimates: genuine download and upload throughput, latency, and the network carrying your traffic, all timed live in your browser in the time it takes to glance at your phone.

    No accounts, no history you didn't ask for, no ads wedged between you and your results. Just a clear, honest number — delivered the way good news should be: quickly, and once.
