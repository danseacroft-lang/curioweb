About HypaSpeed

HypaSpeed is a small, focused tool for one question: how fast is this connection, right now?

No accounts, no history you didn't ask for, no clutter around the number. Just a clean measurement, presented well.

Built as a design showcase — the download reading is a genuine in‑browser transfer test; the interface is what we spent our time on.
