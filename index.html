The Philadelphia Experiment

A long running story claims a US Navy ship was made invisible during a secret wartime experiment involving powerful electromagnetic fields. Later versions add stranger details, including the ship supposedly disappearing and reappearing elsewhere. The US Navy has denied that the experiment took place, and researchers generally trace the legend to later accounts rather than contemporary evidence.

A famous mystery built from wartime secrecy, later testimony and a very strange story.

Honey never really spoils.

Archaeologists have found ancient honey that remained edible after thousands of years. Its low water content and acidic chemistry make it a very unfriendly place for most microbes.

A sweet little bit of science hiding in your kitchen cupboard.
