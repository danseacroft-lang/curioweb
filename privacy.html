Privacy Policy

1. Overview

HypaSpeed ("we", "us", "our") provides a browser-based internet speed test. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the site, why it's collected, and what choices you have. This is a draft document produced during early development and is subject to change.

2. Information We Collect

When you run a speed test, the following information is processed:

  • Network performance data — download speed, upload speed, ping, and jitter, measured directly by your browser during the test.
  • IP address and network details — your public IP address, approximate ISP/network provider, country, and the Cloudflare data center that served your test, retrieved to display connection context.
  • Device and connection type — where available from your browser, general information such as Wi-Fi/cellular/ethernet connection type.
  • Contact form submissions — if you use the "Get in touch" form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide.

3. How We Use This Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Run and display the results of your speed test;
  • Show connection context (such as ISP name and server location) alongside your results;
  • Respond to messages submitted through the contact form;
  • Improve the reliability and accuracy of the testing tool during development.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Local Storage

HypaSpeed stores a small amount of information directly in your browser's local storage, including your recent test history, theme preference, and wallpaper choice. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Third-Party Services

To operate, HypaSpeed relies on the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — used to measure download/upload throughput and to look up IP and network information. Cloudflare's own privacy practices apply to data processed through their speed-test infrastructure.
  • FormSubmit — used to relay contact form submissions by email. Messages you submit are processed by FormSubmit before reaching us.

6. Data Retention

Speed test results and IP lookups are not stored on our servers — they are displayed to you directly and are not retained by HypaSpeed beyond your session, except for the history kept locally in your own browser. Contact form messages are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you (such as a contact form submission). Since this is an alpha-stage placeholder policy, please treat any such request informally by getting in touch — a formal process will be documented in the final policy.

8. Cookies

HypaSpeed does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. Only browser local storage (see Section 4) is used, which is not a cookie and is not shared with third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy

As HypaSpeed moves from alpha to a public release, this policy will be reviewed and replaced with a complete, finalized version. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact form on the homepage.
